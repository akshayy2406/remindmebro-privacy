Privacy Policy — Remind Me Bro: Alarm To Do

Effective date: 15 October 2026
Developer: Akshay Kumar
Contact: remindmebro.care@gmail.com

Remind Me Bro ("the app") is an alarm and reminder app that works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect it.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account, sign-in or server.
  • It does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your phone:

  • Your reminders: titles, times, repeat settings and snooze choices.
  • A history of how each reminder went (done, snoozed, missed), used to show your weekly report and to let Bro refer to past behaviour.
  • Your settings and choices made during setup.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also delete reminders and history inside the app at any time.

Android may include app data in your Google device backup if backup is enabled on your phone. That is controlled by your device settings and Google's backup service, not by us.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsShow the alarm notification.
Full-screen notificationsLet the alarm take over the screen at the time you chose, like a clock alarm.
Exact alarmsFire your reminder at the exact time you set.
Run at startupRestore your alarms after the phone restarts.
Foreground service, wake lockKeep the alarm sounding while the screen is off.
VibrationVibrate when an alarm rings.
Ignore battery optimisation (optional)Stop some phones from suspending the app and missing alarms. Never required.

None of these permissions are used to collect data.

Reading reminder names aloud

If you turn on the optional setting that reads a reminder's name aloud, the app uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine to speak it. The app only uses voices that work offline, so the reminder text is not sent to a server by the app. Which speech engine is installed is your choice and is governed by that engine's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting data, for example by adding analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
